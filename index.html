03Research Output

Current Research

I study how referring expressions in English and Japanese instructional mathematics videos can be grounded to handwritten equations, subexpression spans, terms, figures, and figure parts. I build datasets and evaluation methods for connecting spoken references with handwritten mathematical content, and study how different multimodal models handle ambiguous or overlapping visual candidates.
